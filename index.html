What we run

Four classes, capped at 16

Every session is coached start to finish. Small enough that someone corrects your setup before it becomes a habit.

All levels · 60 min

Barbell Strength

Squat, press, deadlift. Linear progression with a coach checking every rep. If you have never touched a barbell, start here.

Intermediate · 45 min

Conditioning

Intervals, sleds, ropes and carries. Hard for forty-five minutes, then you go home. Scaled every session.

All levels · 50 min

Mat & Mobility

Controlled work for hips, shoulders and the mid-back. The class that keeps the other two available to you.

All levels · 45 min

Row & Ride

Rower and bike intervals with a screen you can ignore. Low impact, high effort, easy on the knees.

How it works

Coached, not supervised

There is a difference between a gym with staff and a gym with coaches. Here someone writes your programme, watches you lift it, and changes it when it stops working.

Your first class is free and there is no sales conversation afterwards. Come to one, see whether you like the room, and decide then.
